## The Unexpected Offer

Cristian received an urgent email from a billionaire friend—a founder and CEO of a large, well-known company—requesting an immediate conversation. The two had previously discussed collaboration opportunities that didn't materialize, but reconnected after the author shared LinkedIn content about his current projects.

The billionaire offered Cristian a high-profile position at his company. It was the kind of opportunity many would consider life-changing: stable employment at a respected organization with significant earning potential.

## The Decision

Despite the compelling nature of the offer, Cristian declined. He explained that he wasn't interested in permanent employment but would consider joint venture collaborations instead. When he told his wife that evening, she was stunned. She expected him to accept or at least consider it more seriously, given his history as a solopreneur and her preference for traditional employment stability.

## The Reasoning

Cristian has extensive experience in large corporations and understands their constraints—"policies and red tape" that often limit flexibility, even with special arrangements. More importantly, he believes he's "onto something much bigger" that requires his full attention and commitment.

## Lessons from AutoSpotting

The author reflected on his open-source project, AutoSpotting, released around 2018-2019. While it generated significant interest from venture capitalists and demonstrated massive adoption (handling millions in annual savings for major clients), Cristian couldn't capitalize on it at the time due to personal circumstances and failed monetization attempts.

When he later joined AWS, he discovered the true scale: AutoSpotting had become essential infrastructure for major companies, yet AWS leadership suppressed its visibility, viewing external tooling as competitive threats.

## The Vision Moving Forward

Today, Cristian is actively building in public—developing AWS Savings Plan automation, NAT Gateway alternatives, and RDS rightsizing tools under the 37signals ONCE model. He's receiving increased recognition from the tech community, evidenced by multiple LinkedIn connection requests after sharing his recent work.

He remains committed to his independent path, stating: "this time I'm prepared to stick with it as long as it takes."
