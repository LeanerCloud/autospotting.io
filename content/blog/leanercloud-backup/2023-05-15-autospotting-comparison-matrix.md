The post introduces a newly added feature comparison matrix designed to clarify distinctions between different AWS auto-scaling approaches.

## Main Message

"Over the years many people asked us to explain the difference between AutoSpotting and standard Autoscaling groups, as well as between AutoSpotting Community edition and the commercial offering on the AWS Marketplace."

The author explains that this comparison resource has been published on GitHub to help users understand how these different solutions compare side-by-side.

## Key Points

- The matrix helps distinguish standard Auto Scaling Groups (ASGs) from AutoSpotting solutions
- It compares the free Community edition with the paid AWS Marketplace version
- The resource is available on the AutoSpotting GitHub repository
- The team invites questions via their Slack channel

## Call to Action

Readers are encouraged to reach out through the LeanerCloud Slack community for additional questions about the comparison or the product offerings.
