This week brought solid progress across multiple fronts. The author shares updates on marketing efforts, product releases, and business development activities.

## LinkedIn Outreach

Cold outreach efforts continued with message refinement. After seeking feedback in online communities about improving marketing approaches, the author received approximately 50 replies covering marketing best practices. Monday focused on implementing these suggestions while engaging directly with prospects.

## EBS Optimizer Release

A significant second marketplace release addressed customer feedback about control and flexibility. Key improvements include:

- Opt-out mode functionality
- Tag-based filtering capabilities
- IO1/IO2 volume conversion controls

The billing model shifted from hourly charges to a one-time per-volume fee (approximately 8.33% annually) better suited to the product's one-off conversion use case. This change eliminated an unintended generous free tier while maintaining competitive pricing.

## Podcast Guest Appearance

The author recorded an interview for Jon Myers' podcast covering AWS cost optimization, hands-on learning versus certifications, audience building, and ChatGPT integration. The episode should launch within weeks.

## Partnership Discussions

Conversations with former colleagues yielded marketing and content improvement suggestions, redirecting development priorities away from GUI work toward immediate website enhancements.

## Savings Calculator

A new calculator tool estimates cost savings for both AutoSpotting and EBS Optimizer using current Spot pricing data and typical adoption percentages. This addresses long-standing customer requests for savings visibility and provides a simpler alternative to planned GUI functionality.

## Business Milestone

Daily revenue reached a record $20+, with AutoSpotting projected to exceed $600 monthly. One major customer accounts for over half current revenue, representing significant savings potential but concentration risk.
