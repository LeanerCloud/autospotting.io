## LeanerCloud GUI Work

The developer spent the week advancing the user interface, sharing a demo video showcasing progress. The interface is taking shape with additional features being considered.

The creator is contemplating "adding support for managing AutoSpotting installations and updates, which is a little tedious at the moment" and welcomes feedback on desired features.

## Podcast News

The LeanerCloud podcast is gaining traction. Several cloud cost management companies have expressed interest in collaboration, with some listeners discovering these companies through episodes and becoming paying customers.

However, the creator is "put[ting] the podcast on hold for the next few weeks" to prioritize product development and sales efforts. The focus is shifting toward driving revenue from their own tools rather than covering unrelated technology topics.

The creator is reopening [Patreon sponsorships](https://www.patreon.com/cristim) for supporters interested in maintaining consistent content production, mirroring earlier AutoSpotting Open Source community support.
