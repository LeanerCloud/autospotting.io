## Savings Calculator Integration on leanercloud.com

The author integrated a savings calculator spreadsheet into the leanercloud.com website, adding polish and visual examples to clarify expectations for visitors.

## AutoSpotting Intro Recording

After long procrastination, the author overcame camera shyness through repeated podcast work. He created slides explaining AutoSpotting's benefits, design decisions, and competitive advantages, then recorded and published a presentation video on YouTube and embedded it on his website.

## New Podcast Episode - Spot Masterclass

The author recorded an hour-long podcast episode drawing on seven years of AWS Spot experience from multiple perspectives—as user, software builder, and AWS Specialist Solutions Architect. The episode covered core concepts, integrations, and third-party tools, though initial listener numbers disappointed him (3 on Anchor, 9 on YouTube), revealing how "outcomes are so much decoupled of their inputs and chance plays a huge role."

## More Active on Twitter

The author began writing longer AWS-focused threads on Twitter, covering learning approaches and cost optimization strategies. He finds threads easier to produce than podcasts and blogs and experimented with tools synchronizing Twitter and LinkedIn content.

## GUI Tool Work

Development commenced on the AutoSpotting GUI tool after weeks of postponement. The author made progress building infrastructure to query regions, list Auto Scaling Groups, and structure collected data, with UI implementation planned for the following week.

## Collaborations and Partnerships

Multiple collaboration discussions advanced, with the author reconsidering advisory work arrangements. He committed to converting lengthy repeat advisory sessions into official paid engagements rather than offering unlimited free consulting.
